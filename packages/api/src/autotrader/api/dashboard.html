Learning loops ?

How Kometa learns a strategy ?

1 · Walk-forwardTake 3 years, try up to 20 parameter sets (every try is recorded), keep the best. Then trade the next 6 months, which it has never seen. Slide forward and repeat.
2 · Judge the unseen monthsAt least 200 out-of-sample trades and a profit factor of 1.3 or more, after spread, slippage and commission.
3 · Luck filterDeflated Sharpe: the more variants tried, the higher the bar. Monte Carlo: worst-case drawdown from reshuffled trades.
4 · RobustnessSmall parameter changes must still work, and the idea must work on at least 3 of 5 markets.
5 · Locked holdoutA final year nobody has touched, opened once per version and only if all else passed.

Most ideas fail here, and that is the point: a failure costs nothing, a false pass costs money. Passing makes a strategy a candidate for shadow trading, not a proven money maker.

What you are looking at

Kometa is running.

Kometa is the machine around trading strategies. It tests them, decides how much each may risk, sends the orders, watches every fill, and demotes or retires strategies that stop working. It has no opinions about markets; the strategies do, and each must prove itself before it touches money.

The path of every trade

1 · StrategySees only closed bars, never the future. Emits a signal: side, entry, a mandatory stop, a target.
2 · AllocatorDecides how much of the risk budget this version gets (by stage and track record) and proposes a size.
3 · Risk gateRecomputes the size itself, runs 9 checks, approves, shrinks or rejects, and signs the answer.
4 · ExecutionActs only on a valid signed decision. Sends the order with its stop, confirms the stop at the broker, records slippage.
5 · MonitorAudits every step, alerts you, and feeds results to the lifecycle, which promotes or demotes versions.

No strategy, allocator or learning loop can get around step 3, and the gate can only make a trade smaller, never bigger.

The risk gate's 9 checks, in order

  1. Is trading allowed (no halt)?
  2. Does the signal have a stop on the correct side, at least 1.5× the spread away?
  3. Independent sizing: lots = money at risk ÷ (stop distance × contract size). It never trusts the allocator's number and never rounds up.
  4. Open risk limits: per trade –, per strategy –, total – of equity.
  5. Same-currency exposure: at most – positions in one currency and direction.
  6. Leverage (at most –× equity in notional) and per-symbol lot caps (gold: – lots).
  7. Margin: free margin stays above –× the margin the trade needs.
  8. News blackout (– min around high-impact events) and no new entries after –.
  9. The version's stage allows trading, within its stage limit.

Halts: the automatic brakes

  • Daily halt at a – loss since the 17:00 New York rollover: closes all positions, resumes the next trading day.
  • Weekly halt at a – loss since the week's open: closes everything, resumes next week.
  • Full halt at a – drawdown from the highest end-of-day equity: closes everything and waits for an owner-signed resume token.
  • Reconciliation halt whenever Kometa's records and the broker disagree: no new entries until they match again.

Halts survive restarts. Losses are measured on every account update, open trades included.

The promotion ladder

  • Candidate: passed offline validation (walk-forward, a locked holdout, Monte Carlo, deflated Sharpe).
  • Shadow: live prices, no money, for 4+ weeks and 30+ signals; results must stay inside the backtest's range.
  • Micro: 0.1% risk per trade for 60+ trades; slippage must stay near the cost model.
  • Live: 0.5% risk per trade; 150+ trades and a rolling Sharpe of at least 1 to scale.
  • Scaled: up to 1%, sized by the allocator.

Demotion is automatic: a drawdown beyond 1.5× the Monte Carlo 95th percentile, a rolling profit factor under 1, slippage over 2× the model, or results drifting outside the backtest's interval. Two demotions in six months retire the version.

Words you will see

R (R multiple)
Result ÷ money risked. −1R = stopped out as planned; +2R = won twice the risk.
Expectancy
Average R per trade. Above zero after costs is the whole game.
Profit factor
Gross wins ÷ gross losses. Above 1 means net positive.
Open risk
What would be lost if every open stop were hit right now.
Drawdown
The drop from the highest equity reached.
Slippage
A fill worse than the price when the order was sent.
Shadow trade
What a strategy would have done, tracked without money.
Spread
Ask minus bid: the cost of crossing the market.
Lot (gold)
1 lot = 100 oz, so a $1 move in gold is $100 per lot (0.01 lot: $1).

About making money

Profits in this demo mean nothing: the prices are simulated and the only strategy is a plumbing test. Kometa exists to find out whether a trading method works, cheaply and safely, before real money depends on it. Real strategies come from you or traders (intake tooling, Phase 10) or from the research loops (Phase 9), and every one must survive validation, shadow and micro before it can scale.
